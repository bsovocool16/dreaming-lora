## Ranked missing citations (verified)

1. **Lin et al., "Continual Learning via Sparse Memory Finetuning"
   (arXiv:2510.15103, Meta FAIR/UW, Oct 2025).** Naive sequential LoRA
   fact-injection: NaturalQuestions F1 drops **71%** (89% full FT; 11%
   sparse memory FT) after ~1K facts. Verified 3-0, verbatim quote.
   THREATENS the naive reading of "LoRA adapter as persistent memory" —
   must be cited and repositioned as the documented failure mode the
   dreaming/decay/shadow apparatus exists to counteract. Also: at-scale
   corroboration of our Track 2 forgetting baseline (headline 6), which
   upgrades our toy finding to a reproduction.
2. **Biderman et al., "LoRA Learns Less and Forgets Less" (TMLR,
   arXiv:2405.09673).** Double-edged must-cite: low-rank capacity limit
   (threatens adapter-capacity assumptions; relevant to rank-as-capacity
   in §3) and intrinsic forgetting mitigation (anticipates the
   bounded-damage framing). CAUTION: the claim "LoRA mitigates forgetting
   better than weight decay/dropout" was **REFUTED 0-3** in verification —
   do not cite Biderman for that.
3. **O-LoRA (Wang et al., EMNLP 2023 Findings, arXiv:2310.14152).**
   Replay-free orthogonal-subspace continual LoRA — the principal
   competing mechanism class to replay/dream-based consolidation.
   Position §7 against it explicitly.
4. **Co2L (Cha et al., ICCV 2021).** Contrastive continual learning:
   rehearsal + representation-consistency objective. Anticipates the
   *strategy* of pairing replay with a representational auxiliary loss;
   our coherence loss differs in target (cross-context type consistency
   vs. preserving old-task features) but the lineage must be acknowledged.
5. **OPLoRA (arXiv:2510.13003).** Protects top singular subspaces of
   frozen weights from LoRA perturbation — mechanistic account of *where*
   LoRA forgetting lives; relevant to our map3 finding that consolidation
   scrambles the base fact code.
6. **EWC-on-shared-LoRA (arXiv:2602.17559).** Fisher-regularized
   continual LoRA — adjacent safeguard family.
7. **Merge-before-Forget (arXiv:2512.23017).** Single unified persistent
   LoRA via orthogonal sequential merges — closest existing system to
   "one adapter as the persistent repository"; §7 positioning required.
8. **SDRL (Chen, Diethe & Flach, arXiv:2006.11234, 2020).** Discriminative
   representation loss for continual learning — Fisher-discriminant-STYLE
   auxiliary objective predates us; our formulation's novelty is the
   cross-context/type-token target and the self-generated dream setting,
   not the loss family. Cite and differentiate carefully in §3.4.
9. **Gomez-Villa et al., semantic drift (arXiv:2407.08536).** Drift
   compensation in exemplar-free CL — the "representations move under
   continued training" problem our coherence term also addresses.
10. **KD-in-continual-learning survey** — for the feature-distillation
    family adjacent to coherence.
